# sessions

The Sessions tab of the sidebar's list column: a `sidebar.panel` contribution (`sessions/panel`, `order` -1 so it comes first). It lists the selected project's sessions that have an open tab, in tab bar order (`src/rules/open-sessions.ts`), then renders the `sidebar.section` contributions under them, such as Inactive sessions. A row shows the session's status mark, title, location (branch or worktree, left out for a folder outside git) and last activity; clicking it opens its tab. The row's menu renames the session in place (Enter or leaving the field keeps the title, Escape drops it), then archives or deletes it through `useSessionActions`, whose failure shows under the row, as does a failed rename (`useRenameSession`), with Retry. With no tab open, the panel says how to start a session, once the tabs have loaded. Until then, and while a project's tabs are listed but their sessions have not arrived, it shows placeholder rows (`ListSection` `loading`). When `useSessionListStatus` says the tabs or the sessions failed, an error with Retry replaces the list ("The sessions could not be loaded.") or, when rows are showing, sits above them ("Sessions may be out of date."). The `session-item`, `session-title` and `session-status` test ids are read by tests.
